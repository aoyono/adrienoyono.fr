Let's talk about 3 workable steps to setup a not so bad pocket technology stack.

What is pocket technology? You remember that little thing you [american pull out 205 times a day](https://claude.ai/share/3a1365f3-23db-4629-aefc-35c8e0a112bd)? That is an amazing piece of high technology in your hand, not [without](https://berc.berkeley.edu/news/where-does-cobalt-used-smartphone-batteries-come) its [dark secrets](https://www.humanium.org/en/the-current-state-of-child-labour-in-cobalt-mines-in-the-democratic-republic-of-the-congo/).

What is a technology stack? Simply the things that get pieced together
to make the raw amazing technology work. In the case of pocket
technology, you need the hardware, and the main software. The third
element in today's stack is a messaging software, and thus you
complete three broad layers of today's technology world known by most
engineers: the physical layer (hardware), the logical layer
(software), and the application layer (also software). The lowest
layer serves as support to the highest levels in turn.

Enough pedantry. My poor man suggested stack to you:
- The hardware: [Pixel 6A](https://www.gsmarena.com/google_pixel_6a-11229.php)
- The OS: [GrapheneOS](https://grapheneos.org/install/web)
- The messaging: [Signal](https://signal.org/download/)

This stack takes away the [infamous
power](https://www.pcworld.com/article/2657861/is-your-phone-secretly-listening-to-you-heres-a-simple-way-to-find-out.html)
of your pocket technology to read your mind or listen to your
conversations. How? Because Google's God like app [Play
Services](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.google.android.gms&hl=en-US)
isn't able to mess with the rock star hardened GrapheneOS (GOS) and
uniquely identify your phone for its voyeurs friends the advertisers,
since on it, it's just yet another Android app without special
permissions. GOS offers the same level, if not higher, of privacy that
iOS gives to Apple fanatics. Which is great, as it truly liberates the
Android, as it is meant to be, owing to its [open
source](https://www.androidauthority.com/aosp-explained-1093505/)
status. On other Android phone brands like Samsung or Huawei, *God
Google Play Services* app is everywhere, sees everything, hear
everything, cannot be uninstalled, can uninstall things on your phone,
and haunt you while you sleep.

Everyone knows Signal messaging app by now. If you are using WhatsApp
(which you do, necessarily trapped in your social network and its
unreasonable stubbornness, just like me), you are using Signal's
absolute badass end to end encryption protocol, which laugh in the
face of attackers and other actors interested by your private
exchanges, by being, again, [open
source](https://github.com/signalapp/libsignal) and even [free
software](https://www.gnu.org/licenses/license-list.html#SoftwareLicenses). Just
picture that: a software claims to be secure, its algorithm is
published for anyone to have a look and try and learn how to break it,
and still no one has managed to (not publicly in any case), after more
than 10 years of existence.

With this pocket technology stack, you have access to high end
technology for less than £200, and you are guaranteed, still, 2 years
of security updates at the hardware level by Google, and at the
software level by GOS (on most recent Pixel phones, we are talking
about 7 years security updates).

There is something sweet about using the devil to free oneself from it.
